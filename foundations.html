Foundations

Language models interpret.
CPS preserves structure.

CPS separates interpretation from state. A model may interpret a conversation, but CPS preserves source, ownership, confidence, privacy, lineage, memory records, and propagation history.

Why This Matters

State should remain inspectable.

When memory becomes durable, it needs more than fluent language output. It needs bounded access, provenance, clear ownership, and a record of how information moved or stopped.

  • Source is preserved instead of flattened into generic recall.
  • Privacy boundaries remain part of the stored state.
  • Lineage shows how later memories relate to earlier ones.
  • Operator inspection remains possible through CPS Studio.

What CPS Holds

Interpretation is not the same as authority.

Language models can help interpret, summarize, and respond. CPS remains responsible for the underlying social-memory records and the rules that govern propagation, containment, mutation, and consequence.

  • Memory records
  • Confidence and sensitivity
  • Relationship-aware routing
  • Propagation history and provenance

Current Position

Bounded prototype, explicit constraints.

CPS is not presented as a full autonomous society engine or a validated model of human behavior. It is an exploratory prototype for testing memory, privacy, propagation, and consequence with restraint.
