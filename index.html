Context Propagation System

Infrastructure for social memory in artificial worlds.

CPS supports environments where conversation has an afterlife. Information can be remembered, retold, withheld, distorted, and traced across a group instead of disappearing after one exchange.

CPS tracks what happens to information after it enters a group: who knows it, where it came from, whether it can move, and how later versions remain traceable.

Persistent AI systems need boundaries and provenance. CPS gives operators a way to inspect how shared information is retained, blocked, repeated, or changed.

Information moves only when a memory becomes socially relevant, finds a viable relationship path, passes privacy and sensitivity checks, and writes a new memory with lineage.

Studio shows who knows what, where it came from, whether it is direct or hearsay, why a route was accepted or blocked, and what lineage was written.

The Social Life of Information

Most AI systems treat conversation as an isolated exchange. CPS treats information as something that can enter a bounded group state: it may be remembered, shared, blocked, distorted, or return later as consequence.

Studio visualizations are used to inspect relationships, opportunity routes, and propagation state.

How information moves

Memory / Event → Signal → Intention → Opportunity → Exchange → Propagated Memory with Lineage

CPS does not move knowledge just because it exists. A remembered event must become socially relevant, pass relationship and privacy checks, and then create a new traceable memory in another actor.

CPS Studio is the proof surface

CPS Studio shows how CPS becomes inspectable: who knows what, where it came from, which relationship could carry it, why an opportunity was accepted or blocked, and what lineage was written after propagation.

Studio01

Subjects and relationships

Inspect actors, identities, memory counts, relationship topology, trust links, one-way bonds, and mutual connections.

Studio02

Opportunities and blockers

See signals, intentions, candidate propagation routes, activation scores, privacy gates, sensitivity thresholds, cooldowns, and loop prevention.

Studio03

Lineage and provenance

Trace a memory back to its source and distinguish direct knowledge, hearsay, repeated rumor, and blocked private information.

Studio04

Demo validation

Run bounded demo profiles that show public propagation, private containment, observer isolation, and deterministic repeatability.
