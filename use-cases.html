Where CPS can be used

Persistence, privacy, and propagation in practice.

Use case 01

Controlled research simulation

Strong research fit

Studying how information changes what agents know or believe.

A bounded simulation for studying how belief, trust, evidence, authority, ambiguity, and social pressure alter what agents know or believe over time.

CPS makes visible where information came from, who received it, whether confidence changed, whether it mutated, and which relationship path made transmission possible.

Why it matters: this is one of the strongest current CPS use cases because it fits the system's core strengths: bounded actors, partial knowledge, propagation, lineage, containment, and inspection.

Use case 02

Organizational communication and training

Strong applied fit

Showing how messages move through teams and institutions.

CPS can support scenarios where messages, assumptions, private concerns, and misunderstandings move through a team or institution.

The purpose is not to claim that CPS teaches empathy directly. The stronger claim is that CPS can make social consequences visible for reflection: who heard what, what changed, who was excluded, what stayed private, and where ambiguity created misunderstanding.

Why it matters: this could support communication training, institutional foresight, leadership scenarios, and policy rehearsal.

Use case 03

Interactive worlds and games

Strong downstream-client fit

Social consequence without scripting every outcome.

CPS can give authored worlds social consequence without hardcoding every outcome. Characters can remember, share, withhold, misunderstand, and react based on what reached them.

A downstream client such as Lelit Distrikt can provide embodiment, atmosphere, animation, and spatial staging, while CPS provides memory, propagation, privacy, lineage, and relationship logic.

Why it matters: the best use is not an uncontrolled autonomous society. The best use is a bounded authored world where information has traceable consequences.

Use case 04

Personal multi-agent assistant / AI council

Future-facing

A future-facing model for inspectable multi-agent advice.

A future-facing application where several specialized agents hold partial context, discuss a user situation from different perspectives, and produce one recommendation with an inspectable decision trail.

CPS could help show what each agent knew, what context shaped its view, what was shared between agents, and how the final recommendation emerged.

Status: this is not current product functionality. It should be presented as an exploratory future direction.

Use case 05

AI safety, privacy, and memory boundaries

Current fit

Inspecting retention, containment, hearsay, and blocked information.

CPS can act as a testbed for inspecting what information is retained, what is blocked, what becomes hearsay, and what remains private.

This is especially relevant for systems where agents should not all share one global memory. CPS can show bounded access, privacy gates, tainting, lineage, and containment behavior.

Why it matters: the value is not that CPS solves AI safety. The value is that it makes some memory and propagation boundaries easier to inspect.

Use case 06

Museums, galleries, and cultural installations

Exploratory applied fit

Artificial communities that remember visitors indirectly.

CPS can support installations where visitor interactions leave traces that later visitors may encounter indirectly.

Instead of resetting after each interaction, the artificial community can retain selected information, transform it, contain it, or let it reappear later through another actor.

Why it matters: this fits museums and cultural institutions because it creates continuity without requiring a fully open-ended autonomous world.

Use case 07

Human-agent and robotics interaction

Exploratory

Reasoning about partial knowledge across multiple actors.

CPS can help reason about partial knowledge, memory handoff, and context boundaries across multiple agents or embodied systems.

CPS is concerned with the social and informational layer: what an actor knows, what it can share, what remains private, and how memory moves across a group.

Use case 08

Writers and worldbuilders

Creative support use

Tracking who knows what inside a fictional group.

CPS can be used as a design tool for tracking who knows what, how rumors spread, and how social consequences emerge across a fictional group.

This is useful for writers, narrative designers, and worldbuilders who need continuity without flattening every character into the same shared knowledge state.
